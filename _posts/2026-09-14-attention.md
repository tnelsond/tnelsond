## Kids on Fire
Liezl came this year from Zambia Africa and held a training after revival week for her style of ministry called **Kids on Fire**. The style of ministry makes it possible for kids to not only encounter the holy spirit but also to be ministers themselves. There's 3 levels or 3 different components which we visualize after the Tabernacle:
- The outercourt (Messy, lively place; games, action songs, memory verses)
- The innercourt (Lower energy, listening to preaching, skits)
- The Holy of Holies (Slow Worship and "presence time" with the Holy Spirit)

{% include figure popup=true image_path="/assets/pics/2026/09/kidsonfiregroup.webp" caption="The group that got together to learn from Liezl." %}

It's simple, but it's a wonderful strategy that prepares the kids for the meat of the word and living out their faith.

{% include figure popup=true image_path="/assets/pics/2026/09/kidsonfire.webp" caption="Us trying out our new method with kids in Rumchek village." %}

### Attention/Presence

Liezl kept teaching on Jesus' words that we must become like little children to enter the kingdom of heaven and I think I understand that now. With my own daughter Gabrielle there is only one thing she really desires and that's to be in my presence. But she's not satisfied to be near me, actually that doesn't matter to her at all, what truely lights up her world is for me to lock eyes with her and give her the attention she desperately needs. If my attention is elsewhere she knows and she is not satisfied. She loves to spend time with me as long as I am engaging with her and focused on her.

{% include figure popup=true image_path="/assets/pics/2026/09/gabriellelaugh.webp" caption="" %}

I think that's what it is to become like a child, to want God's manifest presence; we know that God is present with us always, but that's not the same as when we can feel it, see it, hear it. A child won't settle for a lesser experience, but a child wants that intimacy. May we all not settle for an experience with God that is shallow and God adjacent, but may we ever listen and ever ask for more and more personal touches from the father who readily gives and readily responds to all who seek him.
